About us

Our mission: to keep African food heritage alive by sharing the recipes, cooks and stories behind every dish, so they get cooked, shared and passed on.

Why we started

Taste of Africa started with a simple idea from our founder: good food is meant to be shared.

  • Celebrate the cooks, not just the dishes
  • Make African recipes easy to follow anywhere in the world
  • Show how much the continent has shaped the world's food

A short history of African food

Four moments that shaped what's on the plate today, across Africa and far beyond it.

  1. Home-grown grains

    Sorghum, millet, teff and African rice were all first farmed on the continent.

  2. Trade routes

    Indian Ocean and trans-Saharan trade brought spices to the coast and inland.

  3. New-world crops

    Maize, cassava, chillies and peanuts came from the Americas and became staples.

  4. Global influence

    The diaspora carried okra, rice-growing skills and one-pot cooking into cuisines like gumbo and soul food.
