Chisanyama

  • Southern Africa
  • Serves 4
  • 1 hr
  • Easy

Shisa nyama means "burn the meat" in Zulu: good cuts on the fire, warm sides and the whole community gathered around.

The story

In South Africa's townships, chisanyama (shisa nyama) is both the meal and the place: a grill where you choose your meat and have it braaied on the spot. Spots like Mzoli's in Gugulethu, Cape Town, and Chaf Pozi in Soweto bring together people from all walks of life, often on a Sunday. Expect music, fatty cuts that stay juicy over the open fire, and plates served with pap and chakalaka.

Ingredients

4

Spice mix

  • 10 ml salt
  • 2.5 ml chilli powder
  • 2.5 ml ground cumin
  • 5 ml black pepper
  • 5 ml cayenne pepper
  • 5 ml dried oregano

Braai meat

  • 4 beef chuck chops
  • 4 lamb braai chops

To serve

  • Chakalaka, warmed
  • Pap
  • Fresh bread

Method

1 Mix the spice

Measure out all the spice mix ingredients and mix them well.

2 Light the fire

Light the charcoal or wood and wait until the coals are glowing and covered in white ash.

3 Season the meat

Season the beef and lamb chops generously with the spice mix on both sides.

4 Braai and serve

Braai the chops to your liking, turning them as they brown. Serve with warm chakalaka, pap and fresh bread.
